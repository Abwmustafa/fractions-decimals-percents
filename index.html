Learn: Food Drive

Some middle school students are running a canned food drive for the local food pantry. Their goal is 100 cans each week. So far this week, they have brought in 75 cans.

How can the students show the part of the goal they have reached so far?

One way: a fraction
A fraction shows part of a whole. 75 out of 100 cans is 75100.
Another way: a percent
A percent also shows part of a whole, where the whole is 100. 75 out of 100 cans is 75%.

Summarize: Three Names for One Amount

  1. Fraction → percent: find an equivalent fraction with a denominator of 100.
  2. Percent → decimal: a percent is a number of hundredths. Divide by 100: 75% = 0.75.
  3. To compare or order: write every number in the same form first.

Reflect

Do you like to show part of a whole as a fraction, a decimal or a percent? Why?

See an example answer

I like percents, because a percent always compares with 100. That makes it easy to compare amounts. For example, 3/4 and 0.8 are hard to compare, but 75% and 80% are easy: 80% is more.
